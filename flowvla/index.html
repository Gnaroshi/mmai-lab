CoRL 2026 · Accepted

FlowVLA

Enabling World Dynamics Understanding
through Pluggable 3D Flow Learning

Junhyeong Go1Mingyu Jung1Woobin Im2Jongbin Ryu1
1 Ajou University2 Samsung Electronics
Paper ↗Video ▷BibTeX

1 Overview

2 Abstract

Vision-language-action models inherit strong image–text representations, but manipulating objects requires understanding how the physical world moves in 3D. FlowVLA brings this motion understanding into existing VLA models through training-only supervision from 3D trajectories reconstructed from RGB demonstrations. It couples the future motion of scene points with the robot’s tool motion, connecting scene dynamics to action prediction. The auxiliary heads are discarded at inference, preserving the original model’s inputs and architecture. Evaluations with Seer and π₀.₅ on LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, and real-world manipulation show gains in task success, robustness to unseen conditions, and learning from fewer demonstrations.

3 Method

Reconstruct 3D trajectories from RGB demonstrations, align them to the robot, and select moving points. A point-level head predicts their displacement; a TCP-level head learns to aggregate these motions into the displacement of the robot’s tool-center point.

5 Results

5-a Simulation

Across LIBERO’s four suites, average success rises from 87.3% to 92.4% for Seer and from 96.8% to 97.5% for π₀.₅.

On LIBERO-Plus, without additional fine-tuning, average success rises from 36.1% to 41.1% for Seer and from 57.5% to 59.2% for π₀.₅. Full comparison →

5-b Real World

FlowVLA improves Seer’s success on all seven tasks. Hang Cup rises from 7/15 to 13/15 successes.
