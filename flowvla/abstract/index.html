FlowVLA

Enabling World Dynamics Understanding
through Pluggable 3D Flow Learning

Learning to connect 3D scene motion with robot control.

Overview

Vision-language-action (VLA) models turn images, language instructions, and robot state into actions. Successful manipulation also requires understanding how objects and the robot move together in 3D.

FlowVLA learns from 3D trajectories reconstructed from RGB demonstrations, using future scene-point movement and robot tool movement as supervision. We evaluate it with Seer and π₀.₅ in simulation and on real robots.

Method

FlowVLA reconstructs 3D point trajectories, aligns them to the robot’s coordinate system, and selects moving points. A point-level head predicts their future displacement; a second head learns to combine these motions into the displacement of the robot’s tool-center point (TCP).

The reconstruction model and flow heads are used only during training. Deployment keeps the original VLA inputs and action decoder.

Selected Results

Representative comparisons with Seer. LIBERO-Long tests long-horizon tasks; LIBERO-Plus tests unseen conditions without additional fine-tuning.
